School life

Learning has a rhythm.

Classroom discovery, music, reading, language and shared experiences give every learner more ways to participate and grow.

The wider experience

Confidence grows through doing.

NewDawn’s extracurricular and enrichment programme supports holistic development alongside the academic curriculum.

Learners encounter opportunities to perform, read, speak, collaborate and spend time beyond the classroom. These experiences help develop communication, creativity and a stronger sense of community.

Music

Room to listen, practise and perform.

Dedicated music spaces include a drum set, guitar, violins, violas, cello and digital keyboard. Learners gain exposure to different instruments and the discipline of creative practice.

Music offers another language for expression and another way for learners to discover what they can do.
