About NewDawn School

Holistic, learner-centred education rooted in Kenyan excellence and global readiness.

Overview

Private • CBC-Aligned • Ministry Registered

NewDawn School is a private institution situated on a spacious 2-acre campus along the Bondo–Kisian Highway, just outside Bondo town.

Our academic program is based on the National Competency Based Curriculum (CBC). English is the language of instruction.

Enrichment

Beyond the Classroom

  • Music program: drums, guitar, strings & keyboard.
  • Reading culture: weekly storybook sessions.
  • French: for Grades 4–6.
  • Competitions: spelling bees & debates.
  • Digitized classrooms: smart boards (Junior School).
