About NewDawn School

Holistic, learner-centred education rooted in Kenyan excellence and global readiness.

Overview

Spacious campus. Warm culture. Serious learning.

NewDawn School is a private institution on a 2-acre campus along the Bondo–Kisian Highway. We provide a supportive environment where learners grow in confidence, character and competence.

  • English is the language of instruction for learners and staff.
  • Weekly storybook sessions build a strong reading culture.
  • Spelling bees and debates strengthen communication.
Levels

Clear pathways from early years to Junior School

We offer Playgroup, PP1–PP2, Primary (Grades 1–6), and Junior School.

  • Strong literacy & numeracy foundation in ECD.
  • Structured, supportive learning in Primary.
  • Digitized Junior School with smart boards.
