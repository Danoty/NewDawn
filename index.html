About NewDawn School

We nurture confident learners through quality education and strong values.

Who We Are

A strong foundation for every learner

NewDawn School is a private institution located along the Bondo–Kisian Highway, just outside Bondo town. We serve learners from Bondo Sub-County and surrounding areas.

Our academic structure includes Pre-Primary (Playgroup, PP1 & PP2), Primary (Grades 1–6), and a growing Junior School.

Learning Culture

CBC enriched with creativity and discipline

We fully implement Kenya’s Competency Based Curriculum (CBC) and are registered with the Ministry of Education. English is the language of instruction.

  • Music program with real instruments (strings, drums & keyboard)
  • Weekly storybook sessions to build reading culture
  • French lessons for Grades 4–6
  • Debates and spelling bees for communication skills
  • Planned life-skills programs (e.g. culinary arts)
