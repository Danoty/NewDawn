About NewDawn School

A modern CBC school serving Bondo Sub-County on a spacious 2-acre campus.

Overview

Private School • Founded 2016 • Bondo–Kisian Highway

NewDawn School is a private institution situated just outside Bondo town along the Bondo–Kisian Highway, serving a culturally diverse learner community and supporting holistic growth in academics, character and life skills.

Our learning environment is safe, supportive, and built for excellence — with strong teacher care and a welcoming campus culture.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for parents and guardians.

Which curriculum do you follow?

We implement Kenya’s Competency Based Curriculum (CBC) and are registered with the Ministry of Education.

When was NewDawn School founded?

NewDawn School was founded in 2016 and continues to grow as a holistic learning community.

Do you offer music and languages?

Yes. We offer music lessons (drums, guitar, strings, keyboard) and French classes for Grades 4–6.

How do I start admissions?

Use the Apply/Request Information form, or call/WhatsApp the number on this website to book a tour and start placement.
