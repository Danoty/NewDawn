About NewDawn School

Holistic, learner-centred education rooted in Kenyan excellence.

School Profile

“Do not do what others do better, do it differently.” – Steve Jobs

NewDawn School is a private institution situated on a spacious 2-acre campus along the Bondo–Kisian Highway, serving a culturally diverse learner community.

  • Founded in 2016
  • 400+ learners
  • 18 teachers & 10 non-teaching staff
  • Junior School introduced in 2025

Curriculum & Programs

CBC-aligned and Ministry registered

  • National Competency Based Curriculum (CBC)
  • English as language of instruction
  • Spelling bees & debates
  • French for Grades 4–6
  • Music education (drums, guitar, strings, keyboard)
  • Weekly storybook sessions
  • Planned life skills (culinary arts)

Extracurricular Activities

Supporting holistic learner development
