About NewDawn School

“Do it differently.” A safe, supportive environment for holistic growth.

Overview

A Private School on a 2-Acre Campus

NewDawn School is a private institution situated on a spacious 2-acre campus along the Bondo–Kisian Highway, just outside Bondo town, serving a culturally diverse learner community.

Our staff team includes 18 teachers and 10 non-teaching personnel, committed to a safe, supportive, and high-quality learning environment.

Academics

CBC-Aligned, Ministry Registered

Our academic program is based on the National Competency Based Curriculum (CBC) and is fully registered with the Ministry of Education. English is the language of instruction.

Pre-Primary (Playgroup, PP1, PP2) Strong early years foundation through play, communication, and creativity.
Primary (Grades 1–6) CBC learning with strong literacy, numeracy, and values-based development.
Junior School (introduced 2025) Modern learning supported by digitized classrooms and smart boards.
Enrichment French (Grades 4–6), weekly storybook sessions, spelling bees & debates.
