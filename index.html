About NewDawn School

We do not copy what others do — we do it differently, with excellence.

Overview

A strong foundation for every learner

NewDawn School is a private institution situated on a spacious 2-acre campus along the Bondo–Kisian Highway, just outside Bondo town. We serve a culturally diverse learner community from across Bondo Sub-County.

Our structure includes Pre-primary (Playgroup, PP1 & PP2), Primary (Grades 1–6), and a Junior School introduced in 2025.

Learning & Culture

CBC + Enrichment that builds confident learners

Our academic program is based on Kenya’s Competency Based Curriculum (CBC). English is the language of instruction for learners and staff.

  • Music program with instruments: drums, guitar, violins, violas, cello & digital keyboard.
  • Weekly storybook sessions to foster a strong reading culture.
  • French classes for Grades 4–6.
  • Spelling bees & debates to sharpen language and public speaking.
  • Planned expansion into life skills (e.g., culinary arts).

Junior School (Introduced 2025)

Digitized classrooms and a modern learning experience.

Digitized Learning

Smart boards in every classroom

Our Junior School is fully digitized, with each classroom equipped with smart boards. Learners benefit from engaging visuals, interactive instruction and structured digital learning.

Currently, Junior School has 20 learners as we scale intentionally while maintaining quality.

Safe & Supportive

A campus designed for growth

With over 2 acres, learners enjoy ample space for academics, recreation and co-curricular activities. We are committed to a safe, supportive environment for learners and staff.
