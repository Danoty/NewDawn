About NewDawn School

Modern facilities, strong values, and excellence for every learner.

Who We Are

A Kenyan School with a Global Mindset

NewDawn School is a dynamic learning community in Bondo, Siaya County, nurturing learners through strong academics, character formation, and future-ready skills in a safe environment.

Our approach blends CBC competencies with a strong culture of mentorship, ICT exposure, co-curricular activities, and close parent partnership.

CBC Pathways

Playgroup • PP1–PP2 • Primary • Junior School

Playgroup Gentle transition into learning through play, routines, and social skills.
Early Years (PP1–PP2) Play-based foundation in communication, numeracy, creativity, and confidence.
Primary (Grades 1–6) Strong literacy, numeracy, CBC projects, values and enrichment activities.
Junior School (introduced in 2025) Deeper STEM focus, projects, clubs, guidance and mentorship.
Co-Curricular Sports, arts, music, clubs, environment and leadership development.

Early Childhood

Warm, Safe & Stimulating for Young Minds

Our early years focus on play-based learning, early literacy, numeracy and creativity. Every child is supported to build confidence and independence.

  • ✓ Colourful child-friendly classrooms
  • ✓ Music, movement and creative arts
  • ✓ Outdoor play & exploration
  • ✓ Story time, phonics and communication
  • ✓ Early numeracy through fun activities
  • ✓ Social and emotional support
Learning Areas

What Your Child Will Experience

Language English, Kiswahili, phonics & storytelling.
Numeracy Counting, shapes, patterns & problem-solving.
Creative Arts Drawing, painting, crafts & modelling.
Life Skills Sharing, routines, confidence & independence.
