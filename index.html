About NewDawn School

Holistic learning with strong values, strong language foundation, and modern facilities.

Overview

Spacious campus. Warm culture. Serious learning.

NewDawn School is a private institution on a 2-acre campus along the Bondo–Kisian Highway. We serve the educational needs of Bondo Sub-County and welcome a culturally varied learner body.

  • English is the language of instruction for learners and staff.
  • Weekly storybook sessions build a strong reading culture.
  • Spelling bees and debates strengthen communication and confidence.
Enrollment & Structure

Clear pathways from early years to Junior School

We offer Pre-primary (Playgroup, PP1, PP2), Primary (Grades 1–6), and Junior School (introduced in 2025).

  • Over 230 learners in Early Childhood Development classes.
  • More than 200 learners in Grades 1–6.
  • Junior School is fully digitized with smart boards.
