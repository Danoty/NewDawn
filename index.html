About NewDawn School

Holistic, learner-centred education rooted in Kenyan excellence.

Who We Are

Do it differently

NewDawn School is a private CBC-aligned institution founded in 2016, situated on a spacious 2-acre campus along the Bondo–Kisian Highway, Siaya County.

We nurture learners academically, socially, emotionally, and morally, providing a safe and supportive environment for growth.

School Profile
  • Over 400 learners
  • 18 teachers & 10 non-teaching staff
  • English as language of instruction
  • Junior School introduced in 2025

Curriculum & Programs

CBC-aligned learning with enrichment and future readiness

Early Years (Playgroup, PP1–PP2) Strong literacy, numeracy, and social foundations.
Primary (Grades 1–6) Competency-based learning with structured support.
Junior School Digitized classrooms with smart boards.
Enrichment Music, debates, spelling bees, French, and life skills.
