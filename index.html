About NewDawn School

Holistic, learner-centred education rooted in Kenyan excellence and global readiness.

NewDawn School is a private, CBC-aligned school located along the Bondo–Kisian Highway, just outside Bondo town. English is the language of instruction, and our environment supports joyful learning, care and growth.

Programs & Enrichment

Strong academics + skills, talent and character development.

  • Playgroup, Pre-Primary (PP1–PP2), Primary (Grades 1–6) and Junior School.
  • Music program: drums, guitar, strings and keyboard.
  • Weekly storybook sessions to build reading culture.
  • French for Grades 4–6, spelling bees and debates.
  • Digitized learning with smart boards (Junior School).
