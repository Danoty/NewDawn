About NewDawn School

We serve Bondo Sub-County and beyond, welcoming a culturally diverse learner community and supporting holistic growth in a safe, caring environment.

Overview

Do it differently.

NewDawn School is a private institution on a spacious 2-acre campus along the Bondo-Kisian Highway, just outside Bondo town. We are fully registered and follow the National Competency Based Curriculum (CBC).

We build confident learners through strong academics, values, character, and modern learning tools. Our environment is designed to be welcoming, safe, and supportive for both learners and staff.
