Curriculum & Academic Programs

CBC-aligned, enriched and Ministry registered

Curriculum

The academic program at NewDawn School is based on the National Competency Based Curriculum (CBC) and is fully registered with the Ministry of Education.

  • English is the language of instruction for all learners and staff
  • Structured CBC delivery across all learning levels
  • Strong literacy and numeracy foundation
Enrichment
  • Weekly storybook sessions to foster reading culture
  • French language classes for Grades 4–6
  • Spelling bees and debates for language mastery
  • Planned introduction of life skills, including culinary arts
