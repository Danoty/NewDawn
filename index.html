About NewDawn School

Holistic, learner-centred education rooted in Kenyan excellence and global readiness.

Overview

Private • CBC-Aligned • Ministry Registered

NewDawn School is a private institution situated on a spacious 2-acre campus along the Bondo–Kisian Highway, just outside Bondo town, serving Bondo Sub-County with a culturally diverse learner community.

Our academic program is based on the National Competency Based Curriculum (CBC) and is fully registered with the Ministry of Education. English is the language of instruction.

Curriculum & Enrichment

Beyond the Classroom

  • Music program: drums, guitar, violins, violas, cello & digital keyboard.
  • Reading culture: weekly storybook sessions to foster strong reading habits.
  • Language: French classes for Grades 4–6.
  • Competitions: spelling bees and debates to build confidence & communication.
  • Future skills: plans to introduce more life skills such as culinary arts.
  • Digitized learning: Junior School classrooms equipped with smart boards.

Early Years

Nurturing Confidence from Day One

Our early years program builds communication, curiosity, independence and social skills through guided play, stories, music, movement, and creative activities in a safe environment.

  • Play-based learning + strong routines

  • Gentle support for social & emotional growth
Facilities

Safe, Spacious, Supportive

Dedicated spaces for Early Childhood Development and Primary education, plus specialized music instruction areas. The campus offers ample room for academic, recreational, and extracurricular activities.
