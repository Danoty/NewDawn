About NewDawn School

“Do it differently.” A safe, supportive environment for holistic growth.

Overview

A Private School on a 2-Acre Campus

NewDawn School is a private institution situated on a spacious 2-acre campus along the Bondo–Kisian Highway, just outside Bondo town, serving a culturally diverse learner community. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

Our staff team includes 18 teachers and 10 non-teaching personnel, committed to a safe, supportive, and high-quality learning environment. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

Academics

CBC-Aligned, Ministry Registered

Our academic program is based on the National Competency Based Curriculum (CBC) and is fully registered with the Ministry of Education. English is the language of instruction for learners and staff. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

Pre-Primary (Playgroup, PP1, PP2) Strong early years foundation through play, communication, and creativity.
Primary (Grades 1–6) CBC learning with strong literacy, numeracy, and values-based development.
Junior School (introduced 2025) Modern learning supported by digitized classrooms and smart boards. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}
Enrichment French (Grades 4–6), weekly storybook sessions, spelling bees & debates. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}
