Website information

Privacy at a glance.

This static website is designed to minimise collection of personal information.

Enquiry forms

The admissions and contact forms create a pre-filled WhatsApp message on your device. The website itself does not transmit the form to a NewDawn database or store your entries. You choose whether to send the message after WhatsApp opens.

External services

Links to WhatsApp, email and Google Maps take you to services operated by other organisations. Their own privacy terms apply. The site may also request typefaces from Google Fonts when available.

Media and children

The website uses school-supplied photographs and videos. The school should maintain appropriate permissions for publishing identifiable learners and review media whenever circumstances change.

Future additions

If analytics, online applications, payments, accounts or a content-management platform are added, this notice should be replaced with a school-approved privacy policy describing the responsible organisation, lawful basis, retention, safeguards and contact route.

Publishing check: This is practical website information, not a substitute for a formal policy reviewed against applicable Kenyan data-protection requirements.
